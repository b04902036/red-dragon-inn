# Server-owned deadlines and phase-end grace

Step 21B extends the same 30-second system to pending ante/payment, gambling checkpoints, pre-payout settlement, committed Fortitude loss, and Order Drink Sometimes opportunities. These use server-private legal eligibility and existing alarms/replay. See [generic capabilities](rdi1-engine-capabilities.md#timing-and-verification).

For new matches, other players' response decisions last 30 seconds and phase-end Anytime decisions last 15 seconds. The active turn owner has no deadline for either decision: they can still play any currently legal Sometimes/Anytime card until explicitly passing. Normal turn phases also wait for the owner's action. The fixture browser environment injects 10/5 seconds for other players. No client option changes a deadline.

The match's pinned rules carry `timing.turnOwnerUntimed: true`. Older persisted manifests without that flag retain their original all-player deadlines so saved history replays identically. Start a new match to use the updated policy.

System response eligibility includes legal Anytime holders even without a matching Sometimes. These use the same owner-aware source-response policy; private `hasLegalSometimes` remains false and the Sometimes voice stays silent. Anytime alone does not create an Order Drink Sometimes opportunity. Card legality and response priority still apply during untimed decisions.

The persisted control state stores `timedPrompt` (immutable ID, kind, window ID, priority player, opening time and nullable deadline) and a phase-end continuation. `deadlineAt: null` identifies an untimed owner decision; its prompt identity and private legal plays remain available. One clock reading is supplied per accepted server transaction. Only the authoritative priority holder receives private `responsePrompt` with `hasLegalSometimes`; legal cards and targets stay private. Public state exposes the current countdown/priority, without eligible-hand lists.

Discard/Draw, Action, Order Drink and Drink finish their special action or complete resolution before opening grace. The original phase remains visible until every living holder of a legal Anytime passes. Players without one are skipped. `PASS_ANYTIME` names the phase-end window; normal turn actions cannot bypass grace. An Anytime play suspends grace through all nested response windows, clears its pass consensus and then recomputes from the active-player origin with fresh identities and deadlines. Elimination remains suspended through pending resolution and this grace, allowing last-chance healing.

`GameRoom` commits game state, accepted history/outbox and the next Durable Object alarm in one storage transaction. Untimed decisions have no gameplay alarm and reject `EXPIRE_PROMPT`, including stale alarms and reconnect reads. Finite deadlines use the separate `EXPIRE_PROMPT` system boundary; the WebSocket command schema cannot accept it or reserve a `command_system_` identity. Expiry produces deterministic timeout/pass events, advances version, persists to D1 and broadcasts normal projections. A failed D1 mirror retains the durable outbox and schedules a retry. Early/stale alarms cannot pass a newer prompt. Reconnect and hibernation retain the original deadline or untimed identity.

Replay stores the accepted clock reading alongside ordinary commands and the actual expiry time in system actions. It regenerates and verifies all events without consulting the current wall clock. The browser's countdown is presentation only. Optional prompt IDs and authoritative state/window/priority checks reject stale intents.

The English MP3 at `/audio/voice/en-US/sometimes-response.mp3` is shared by English and Traditional Chinese interfaces. A new local response prompt with a legal Sometimes card consumes a `voice:promptId` key once. Locale changes, rerenders, refresh and reconnect do not replay it. Anytime-only, phase-end and remote prompts do not speak. The voice follows the existing SFX volume/mute controls; missing playback cannot block music or gameplay. See [audio provenance](../public/audio/LICENSES.md).

For visual verification, run `npm run dev:fixture` and create/join a room in two separate browser profiles. Discard/draw: the phase remains Discard/Draw while the owner sees the no-time-limit message and Pass Anytime. Wait beyond five seconds, then play Quiet Breather; the owner's response also remains untimed beyond ten seconds. Explicitly pass to another eligible player to see their countdown and automatic timeout. Pass remaining responses/grace, then play Friendly Shove and respond with Brush It Off: finite countdowns should restart with new prompt identities. Switch language/reconnect during a prompt: its deadline or untimed status stays fixed and the voice does not repeat. Production uses 30/15 seconds for other players rather than the fixture's 10/5.
