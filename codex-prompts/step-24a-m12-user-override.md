# 1. Authoritative M12 User Decisions

The following decisions are now authoritative for this project.

## M12-OV1 — Gog has two ante-avoidance effect templates

Treat Gog's ante-avoidance cards as having these two effect templates.

### Template A

```text
Avoid the current ante
and leave the current Round of Gambling.
```

### Template B

```text
Choose the applicable branch:

A. Avoid the current ante
   and leave the current Round of Gambling.

OR

B. Ignore the current Drink.
```

For project behavior, Template B is confirmed to contain the `Ignore Drink`
alternative.

Do not leave this unresolved.

---

## M12-OV2 — Ante-avoidance cards may respond to later antes

Gog's ante-avoidance effects are NOT limited to the initial ante.

If Gog is still participating in a Round of Gambling and becomes required to
ante again, including because another player plays:

```text
I raise!
```

Gog may immediately play an applicable ante-avoidance card in response to that
ante requirement.

Therefore this sequence is LEGAL:

```text
Gog is participating
→ another player plays I raise!
→ Gog becomes required to ante
→ Gog plays an ante-avoidance card
→ Gog does not pay that current ante
→ Gog leaves the Round of Gambling
```

This is an explicit user-approved M12 rule.

Do not block on publisher evidence for this interaction.

---

# 2. Ante Trigger Model

Model Gog's ante-avoidance branch as responding to an actual ante requirement.

Conceptually:

```text
systemEvent: ANTE_REQUIRED
actor: GOG / SELF
```

It must work for both:

```text
INITIAL_ANTE
```

and:

```text
LATER_ANTE
```

including later antes generated by:

```text
I raise!
```

Do not restrict it based on whether the ante is the first ante of the Round.

---

# 3. Ante-Avoidance Resolution

When Gog legally uses the ante-avoidance branch:

```text
1. Cancel the current ante requirement for Gog.
2. Do not remove Gold from Gog for that ante.
3. Gog leaves the current Round of Gambling.
4. Gold Gog already placed in the pot remains in the pot.
```

The effect cancels only the ante Gog is currently being required to pay.

It does not refund previous antes.

---

# 4. Consequences of Leaving the Round

After Gog leaves the Round of Gambling:

```text
Gog is no longer an active gambling participant.
```

For the remainder of that Round:

- Gog cannot play Gambling cards.
- Gog cannot play Cheating cards.
- Gog may still play otherwise-legal Sometimes cards.
- Gog may still play otherwise-legal Anytime cards.
- Gold Gog previously anted remains in the pot.
- Gog is not affected by any subsequent effects requiring participants to ante.
- This explicitly includes future `I raise!` effects.

Example:

```text
Gog ante = 1 Gold
Gog remains in Round

Player A: I raise!
Gog uses ante-avoidance
→ Gog pays 0 for this raise
→ Gog leaves
→ Gog's original 1 Gold remains in pot

Player B later: I raise!
→ Gog is already out
→ Gog pays 0
→ no response card is required
```

---

# 5. Template A Behavior

Template A is:

```text
Avoid current ante
+
Leave Round of Gambling
```

It is legal whenever:

```text
Gog is currently participating
AND
Gog is currently required to ante
```

This includes:

- the initial ante;
- an ante caused by `I raise!`;
- any other future mechanic that directly creates an ante requirement for
  active participants, unless that specific mechanic explicitly forbids
  responses.

Resolution:

```text
CANCEL_CURRENT_ANTE_FOR_SELF
LEAVE_GAMBLING
```

---

# 6. Template B Behavior

Template B has two mutually exclusive context-dependent branches.

## Branch A — Ante avoidance

When:

```text
Gog is currently participating
AND
Gog is required to ante
```

Gog may use:

```text
Avoid current ante
+
Leave Round of Gambling
```

This follows exactly the same ante behavior as Template A.

---

## Branch B — Ignore Drink

When a Drink is currently affecting Gog, Gog may instead use:

```text
Ignore the current Drink
```

Apply the project's normal Ignore Drink rules.

If the engine models Chasers according to standard RDI rules, ignoring the
Drink must use the existing shared Ignore Drink resolution rather than a
Gog-specific duplicate implementation.

Do not apply the ante-leaving effect when Gog chooses the Drink branch.

The two branches are context-sensitive alternatives, not simultaneous effects.

---

# 7. Important Distinction: Leaving vs. Responding

Implement both cases correctly.

## Case A — Gog is still participating

Example:

```text
I raise!
→ Gog is required to ante
```

Gog MAY respond with an ante-avoidance card.

If he does:

```text
avoid this ante
→ leave Round
```

---

## Case B — Gog already left

Example:

```text
Gog previously used ante-avoidance
→ Gog already left

Later:
I raise!
```

Gog is not affected by the ante at all.

No ante-avoidance card is needed.

Do not open an unnecessary ante-response window for Gog after he has already
left the Round.

---

# 8. Do Not Refund Previous Antes

This rule is mandatory.

Example:

```text
Initial ante:
Gog pays 1 Gold.

Later:
I raise!

Gog uses ante-avoidance.
```

Result:

```text
Gog does NOT pay the new ante.
Gog leaves the Round.
The original 1 Gold remains in the pot.
```

Incorrect behavior:

```text
refund all Gog ante Gold
```

Do not implement that.

---

# 9. Reaction / Priority Requirements

The engine must expose an ante-response opportunity before automatically
deducting Gog's Gold.

Correct order:

```text
ANTE_REQUIRED created
→ determine eligible responses
→ Gog may play ante-avoidance
→ if no applicable response/pass:
     collect ante
```

Incorrect:

```text
deduct Gold
→ then ask whether Gog wants to avoid ante
```

If the current engine deducts ante immediately, refactor the ante flow enough
to support this response timing generically.

Do not create a hardcoded `if character === Gog` workaround if the engine
already has or should have a generic ante-response mechanism.

---

# 10. Interaction With I Raise!

When `I raise!` resolves:

```text
1. Apply its normal Gambling control behavior.
2. Generate an ANTE_REQUIRED event for each active participant that must ante.
3. For each applicable player, allow legal responses before payment.
4. Gog may use either Gog ante-avoidance template if one is available.
5. If Gog avoids:
   - do not collect Gog's ante;
   - remove Gog from active gambling participation.
6. Continue resolving the remaining participants correctly.
```

Gog leaving must not cancel the entire `I raise!`.

Other active participants must still ante normally unless they have their own
legal responses.
