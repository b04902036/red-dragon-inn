## Important current-rule overrides

- A Sometimes card is legal only when its stated triggering event directly applies. Changing a Drink is not the same as directly changing a player's Alcohol Content.
- Ignore affects only the protected player; Negate prevents the source from resolving.
- Response order begins with the player who played/revealed the source, then proceeds in turn order; after a response resolves, the original source is offered again.
- `Winning Hand`-style control may only be overtaken by Cheating until a Cheating card does so.
- Cards that pay/ante using the Inn replace the payment at the moment the payment is required; they cannot reclaim Gold already in the pot.
- The 'house takes the pot' gambling response has the current restrictions documented in the official rules.
- The Drink-change counter only counters effects that actually change a Drink; it does not counter ordering drinks, forcing drinking, direct Alcohol-stat effects, Drink Events, or itself.
