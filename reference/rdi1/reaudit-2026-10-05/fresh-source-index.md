## Evidence policy

- Current official rules override old card wording and secondary tables.
- Later official examples may clarify RDI1 card behavior.
- The complete community matrix is used for per-character quantity completeness, not as higher authority than current official rules.
- A matching 40-card total is never treated as proof that an individual effect is correct.
